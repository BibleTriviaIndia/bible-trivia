📖 Bible Trivia

Welcome to Bible Trivia — a simple place to test your knowledge of the Bible and learn more about God's Word.

✝️ What We Offer

- 📚 Bible Questions
- 🧠 Multiple-choice quizzes
- 📖 Bible knowledge challenges
- 🏆 Scores and achievements
- 🙏 Learn and grow in God's Word

🎯 Goal

Our goal is to make learning the Bible simple, enjoyable, and engaging for everyone.

📝 How to Play

1. Choose a Bible quiz.
2. Read each question carefully.
3. Select your answer.
4. Submit your answers.
5. Check your score and learn from the results.

❤️ Support
